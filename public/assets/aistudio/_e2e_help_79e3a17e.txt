usage: smart_pinterest_agent.py [-h] [--max-pins MAX_PINS] [--scrolls SCROLLS]
                                [--download] [--sync-supabase] [--notify]
                                [--watch] [--interval INTERVAL] [--login]
                                [--login-timeout LOGIN_TIMEOUT]

Smart Self-Learning Pinterest Watcher Pipeline (Wallix)

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --max-pins MAX_PINS   max NEW pins to process per board (default 5)
  --scrolls SCROLLS     scroll iterations for the headless fallback (default
                        3)
  --download            download best-quality videos into downloads/ via yt-
                        dlp
  --sync-supabase       push video metadata straight to Supabase
  --notify              also POST each item to receiver_agent.py (port 5000)
  --watch               auto-watch mode: keep re-checking boards
  --interval INTERVAL   seconds between auto-watch checks (default 600 = 10
                        min)
  --login               open the persistent browser for a MANUAL login (10-15
                        min)
  --login-timeout LOGIN_TIMEOUT
                        seconds to keep the browser open for login (default
                        900 = 15 min)
